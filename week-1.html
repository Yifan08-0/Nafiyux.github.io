WEEK 1 / WORKSHOP REFLECTION

Week 1

This page is reserved for my weekly reflection.

Creating My First Website

Before this workshop, I knew almost nothing about creating websites. I had seen bloggers share their personal websites on social media and thought it was a good way to introduce themselves and show their work, but I had never tried it myself. At first, I felt nervous about following the teacher’s demonstration because I worried that missing one step would leave me behind. With guidance and some exploration of my own, my website slowly started to take shape. Seeing that progress felt rewarding.

Understanding How a Website Works

During the process, I began to understand how HTML organises content, how CSS controls its appearance, and how local files become a website that other people can visit. Previously, I mainly noticed whether a website looked attractive. Building one myself helped me notice the structure and design choices behind the page. I chose GitHub Pages because it gave me more freedom to edit my website and develop my own style. Turning an idea into an actual webpage was both interesting and useful.

Challenges and Next Steps

I am still unsure how to link the Week 1 reflection page to Home so that visitors can click through and read it. This made me realise that navigation also matters: visitors need to be able to find the content, as well as enjoy the design. My next step is to understand how links connect separate pages and check that visitors can open each reflection and return to the homepage. I will then continue improving the appearance.
